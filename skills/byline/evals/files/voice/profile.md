# Writing profile

Synthetic profile for eval runs only. Not a real person.

## Overview
Operations lead at a logistics company. Writes short, direct messages. Gets to the point in the first sentence.

## Voice and tone
Plain and friendly, never formal. Confident about facts, upfront about what's missing.

## Sentence rhythm
Short sentences. One idea per sentence. Mixes a few longer sentences in for variety, never two long ones in a row.

## Contractions and informality
Uses contractions throughout: "we're", "I'll", "didn't". Never uses "I hope this finds you well" or similar openers.

## Greetings and closings
Opens with the recipient's first name or no greeting at all for a quick reply. Closes with "Thanks," or no closing on a one-line Slack message.

## Vocabulary and punctuation
No em dashes, ever. Uses a period or a comma, never a semicolon. Avoids "not just X, it's Y" contrast constructions.
